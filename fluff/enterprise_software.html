3 INT. ENTERPRISE SATELLITE OFFICE - AFTERNOON

The dingy office room is lit with a mix of white fluorescent light and diffuse daylight from the high rise windows. The cityscape of Charlotte, North Carolina can be seen outside the windows. In the center of the office is a large rectangular table, where various employees sit with business-class logoless white-label grey laptops.

THOMAS (early 40s, expressionless) sits down in his chair with SPONSORED DRINK in hand. In a single deft motion, he takes a keycard out of his shirt pocket and inserts it into the side of his laptop. He moves and clicks his logoless white-label wireless mouse, then leans back in his chair, taking a sip of SPONSORED DRINK. After a few moments, the laptop logs in. In the center of the laptop screen is a MODAL DIALOG BOX (rounded rectangular, light grey, shaded) with BUTTON 1 (centered, left), BUTTON 2 (centered, right) and CLOSE BUTTON (right corner, small, solid dark grey circle).

MODAL DIALOG BOX: "Press big, shiny button to make your life harder?"

BUTTON 1: "Cancel"

BUTTON 2: "Yes, please"

Without a moment of hesitation, THOMAS grabs his mouse again and slowly but deliberately drags the mouse cursor to BUTTON 2 and clicks it.
